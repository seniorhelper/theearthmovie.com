What is EARTH?

A funny, honest look at a planet that forgot what matters.

EARTH is a satirical feature film about a backwards world where everyone is hooked on their feeds and scored for everything they do. One guy wakes up. What follows is a wild trip through the noise to find the people who are still real.

Underneath the jokes is a simple lesson: love and kindness are worth more than money or stuff. It is made for the whole family, with grown-up allegories for anyone who recognizes themselves in the joke.
