Waitlist privacy

When you join the waitlist, we collect your name, your email address and any interests you choose to share. We use them only to reply to you and to send news about EARTH. We never sell your information. The form is delivered by a third-party form service, which processes your submission to pass it along to us.

Want off the list or want your details deleted? Reply to any email we send and ask. We will remove you.

AI disclosure

EARTH uses AI-generated characters, voices and imagery. Any resemblance to real persons is coincidental.

Ideas, scripts and talent

Please do not send scripts, treatments or story ideas through this site. Unsolicited creative material is not reviewed and creates no obligation. This is standard studio practice that protects both sides.

Actors and creators are welcome to share links to public reels or portfolios. Submissions are reviewed at our discretion, and we may not reply to everyone. Nothing on this site is a promise of a role or of pay.

Partnerships
